# Circulation may run while an Exterior Opening pauses the plant

The Whole-Home Exterior Opening Gate stops heating and cooling while any exterior opening is open, because conditioning a leaky envelope wastes energy and rarely reaches its target. It also stopped Circulation, and that part is not implied by the argument: with a window open and its interior door open, outdoor air is the cooling source and the blower is what distributes it through the house. Circulation is therefore allowed through the gate and left to the same arbitration that decides it everywhere else — opposing comfort errors, or a spread wide enough between occupied rooms in the cold-start rules, and the plan's mix benefit against fan energy once the MPC is live — rather than being forced on whenever an opening is open, which would run the blower in January and contradict the intent.

Two alternatives were considered and dropped. A free-cooling trigger (circulate whenever the house is above its cooling target and outdoor air is materially cooler) cannot be valued by the model at all: the thermal model has no outdoor-air ingress term, so the planner would be acting on a guess, and it would need a margin to avoid running in winter. Always circulating while paused is the simplest to explain and the worst for energy, and contradicts "when it would help equalize temperatures".

Consequences worth stating. In a uniformly warm house the plan still picks idle, because Circulation only moves rooms toward the House Reference Temperature and costs fan energy; the free cooling happens at the opening, and the blower is not asked to help. Deliberate, and the reason this is a small change rather than a feature. Circulation still yields to the Wet-Coil Lockout: the gate's early return must not bypass it, which is harmless today only because it returns idle. The room behind the opening has to take part in that arbitration, and the occupancy layer drops an open-window room to zero priority *and* zero occupancy, which would hide it from the very comparison it is part of: while the gate is active its occupancy probability is read as one for the mixing set only, and its priority stays zero, so it still cannot drive heating or cooling. No door condition is added of its own — under the default `pause_unless_door_closed` a room whose interior door is confirmed closed never pauses the house, so an active gate already implies an open air path somewhere. `pause_always` and `exclude_room_only` keep their meaning apart from allowing Circulation.

Left to the implementation: the guard returns `{idle, circulating}` with no forced action instead of `{idle}` plus forced idle, and only when the coil is not locked out; heating and cooling stay out of the set; the Guard chip and the "rooms paused" count keep their wording, where "paused" now means heating and cooling; `docs/features.md` and the README say "pauses heating and cooling" rather than "pauses the plant"; tests cover the legal set, the forced-action change, the wet-coil interaction, and a coordinator cycle where two rooms disagree behind an open window (circulates) versus a uniform warm house (stays idle).
