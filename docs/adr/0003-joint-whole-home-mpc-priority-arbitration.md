# Joint whole-home MPC with occupancy-priority arbitration

Independent per-room controllers, post-hoc equipment groups, and hydronic radiator afterglow are replaced by a single coupled multi-room forward rollout over discrete Central Plant actions (`idle`, `heating`, `cooling`, `circulating`) weighted by live and forecasted Room Priority, using symmetric forced-air air-vs-mass relaxation (`beta_f`) for both furnace heating and heat-pump cooling. A ducted central plant conditions every open register simultaneously, so only a joint rollout that advances all room temperatures and `T_house` together can weigh over-conditioning one occupied room against under-conditioning another and recognize when Circulation equalizes opposing room errors at a fraction of compressor or furnace energy.
